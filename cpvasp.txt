Dear CP-VASP users,
 We are excited to release CP-VASP 2.0! Here are some key features:
1. Support both vaspsol and vaspsol++
2. Flash solvation method to avoid flying water problem in MD
3. "True" grand canonical ensemble for electron dynamics
The patch file can be accessed here: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/13qTSTIRgWM6RhvcxgeRISVitz7z8jJLr?usp=drive_link
The user manual and examples are available at 
https://github.com/yuanyue-liu-group/CP-VASP/tree/main
For any questions or feedback, please report at 
https://github.com/yuanyue-liu-group/CP-VASP/issues
Sincerely, Yuanyue Liu group The University of Texas at Austin https://sites.utexas.edu/yuanyue-liu/
